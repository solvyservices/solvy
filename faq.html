How much does a repair cost?

Most repairs fall between $120 and $450 including parts and labor. The service call is $89, waived if you go ahead with the repair. You get an exact price before any work starts. See typical prices.

Can you come today?

Often, yes. Same-day slots go fastest in the morning, so call before noon for the best chance.

What warranty do I get?

Every repair is covered for 90 days on parts and labor.

Is it worth repairing an old appliance?

A common rule: if the appliance is past the middle of its life and the repair costs more than half the price of a new one, replacing may make sense. Our technician will tell you honestly which option is better.

Which brands do you repair?

All major brands, including Whirlpool, GE, Samsung, LG, Frigidaire, Maytag, KitchenAid and Bosch. Full list.

Do you repair commercial equipment?

Yes. We work with restaurants, laundromats and property managers. Commercial service.

What areas do you cover?

Orange, Seminole, Osceola and south Lake County. Check your ZIP code.

Do I need to be home during the visit?

Usually yes, or someone over 18 who can let the technician in and approve the quote. You choose a 3-hour arrival window when you book.

How do I reschedule?

Call or text (321) 379-7676. There’s no fee to reschedule.

Are you affiliated with the manufacturer?

No. SOLVY is an independent repair company. We service all major brands using quality parts. If your appliance is still under the manufacturer’s warranty, check with the manufacturer first.

How do I pay?

Payment is due when the repair is complete. Ask about payment options when you book.
