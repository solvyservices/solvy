Stoves & ovens: common questions

I smell gas near my stove. What should I do?

Leave the house now, don’t switch any lights or appliances on or off, and call your gas company or 911 from outside. Call us after they say it’s safe.

My oven takes forever to preheat.

On gas ovens that’s usually a weak igniter; on electric ovens a failing bake element. Both are common repairs.

Do you repair high-end ranges?

Yes, including brands like Dacor, Bosch and KitchenAid.
